Trifle with Nutella and Ferrero Rocher

This recipe is adapted from "Anna in the Kitchen" written by annainthekitchen. And found on https://annainthekitchen.com/nutella-ferrero-rocher-trifles/

An airy Nutella mousse layered with rich brownies and roasted hazelnuts.The word ‘trifle’ is derived from the old French word ‘trufle’ which means whimsical because it was usually thrown together at the last minute. Originating in England and known for it difinitive layers.

Ingredients List

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup nutella, plus an additional ½ cup
  • 4 cups crumbled brownies (homemade, store bought, or from a box mix)
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • ½ cup chopped roasted hazelnuts

Directions

  1. Add the heavy cream to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment. Beat on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form.
  2. Add the nutella and whisk just until combined. Set aside.
  3. Place the nutella in a small, microwave safe bowl. Microwave for 30 seconds, or until the nutella is warm and pourable.
  4. Place ½ cup of crumbled brownie in the bottom of each glass.
  5. Next, layer over ¼ cup of nutella whipped cream, followed by a drizzle of melted nutella, and 1 tablespoon of chopped hazelnuts.
  6. Repeat each of the layers again.
  7. Top with ¼ cup whipped cream.
  8. Serve immediately.
